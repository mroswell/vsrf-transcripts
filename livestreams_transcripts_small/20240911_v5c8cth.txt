Pregnant and breastfeeding women were excluded from the pivotal clinical trials.
And when Pfizer finally commenced these trials, they were abandoned due to a lack of participation.
But this didn't dampen the enthusiasm of authorities who continued to provide baseless safety assurances to women during their most vulnerable time.
