I'm an orthopedic surgeon from Milwaukee, Wisconsin. I was injured by the Moderna shot that I received in December 30th of 2020. Unfortunately, since that time, I developed transverse myelitis, which is an injury to my spinal cord. Unfortunately, because of that, I haven't been able to return to work in my really beloved profession of orthopedic surgery. I'll be honest with you, I didn't think about it enough. I trusted the federal healthcare agencies that were supposed to protect me. I was naive. I admit that. My world and my mind has changed. My vision of how I trust people like the NIH and FDA and CDC has dramatically changed.
