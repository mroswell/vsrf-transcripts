The first individuals who died in gas chambers in Germany were not individuals in concentration camps. They were not ethnic minorities like Jews. That came later. The first gas chambers were found in psychiatric hospitals in Germany, and the first people gassed were medically, physically, and mentally disabled patients. It was physicians signing off on that euthanasia program of, quote, unquote, defective individuals. Physicians who believed they were serving some good. Believed they were serving the greater good. That's exactly right.
