I am also an orthopedic surgeon. Helping people has always been my calling. On December 30th of 2020, I received my first Moderna shot. Approximately one week later, my feet were numb. Within days, my legs became weak. I began to stumble, and ultimately, I collapsed at work. Shortly after my collapse, I was diagnosed with transverse myelitis. A severe and potentially permanent injury to my spinal cord. You see me standing here today, but what you don't see is the numbness, the weakness, and the nerve pain that I have had to endure over the past year. Because of the severity of my symptoms, I was forced to face the reality that I was no longer safe to work as a surgeon. My career that I had invested 19 years of my life was likely over. Days of supporting my patients and my community were replaced with isolation, disability, and doctor visits that seemed to go nowhere. My adverse event was life-altering. I was devastated. Without the loving support of my wife, my four amazing children, and my close friends, I would have fallen into a dark place. Finding love and support is essential for me and my vaccine-injured friends. Those of you here today, your support is essential, too. During this journey, I had the true blessing of meeting Brianne Dressen, whose life was altered by AstraZeneca. And I also met children like Maddie DeGerry, who is now confined to a wheelchair, and parents like Ernest Ramirez, who lost his son. Their horrific and heroic journeys touched my heart. I discovered one essential cornerstone of healing, the realization that I am not alone. I am one of many thousands.
